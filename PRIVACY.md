# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-11-08*

RawCull doesn't collect, send or sell any data.

- **No network access.** The app doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it can't send
  anything off your device. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or accounts.
- **Your photos stay where you put them.** RawCull reads and writes only the folders and SD cards
  you grant it through Android's system file picker, and you can revoke that access at any time.
- **What it stores.** RawCull writes your ratings, labels, tags and locations into XMP sidecar
  files next to your photos. On your device it keeps a catalog database, a preview cache and your
  settings, which are removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Locations.** RawCull shows GPS coordinates that your camera recorded, and the ones you enter
  yourself. Opening a location in a map app hands those coordinates to that app.
- **Android backup.** If Android's device backup is turned on, Android may include RawCull's
  settings and catalog in your backup, under your backup provider's privacy terms. Your photos are
  not part of it.
- **Permissions.** Notifications show import and export progress. The foreground service
  permission keeps an import running while the app is in the background.

Questions go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/shehransyed/RawCull/issues).
